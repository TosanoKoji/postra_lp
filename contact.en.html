Contact

For bug reports, pre-purchase questions, or feature requests.

Before reaching out

Many questions are already answered in the FAQ. Please take a look first — it may resolve your question faster.

For bug reports, the following information helps us investigate quickly:

  • macOS version (e.g. macOS 14.5) and Mac model (e.g. MacBook Pro M2)
  • Postra version (menu bar > About Postra)
  • The steps that lead to the issue
  • Screenshots, if possible

Replies

  • We typically reply within 2–3 business days (excluding weekends, public holidays in Japan, and the year-end holiday period).
  • Some inquiries may take up to about a week.
  • Postra is operated by an individual; we may not be able to reply to every inquiry. Thank you for your understanding.
  • Supported languages: Japanese and English only.

Please note before reaching out

  • Feature requests are not promises. We review and prioritize them based on impact and feasibility.
  • For issues caused by third-party services that the Service depends on (CLIP / DINOv2 and similar AI models, Hugging Face's model distribution, Apple's Mac App Store, Cloudflare's hosting, and others), our ability to assist may be limited. For inquiries about Mac App Store purchases, refunds, or payments, please contact Apple support directly.
  • For business inquiries — sales, advertising, press, partnerships — please add [Business] to the subject line.
