02 · Similarity search + keyword search

"That kind of feel" —
found, just like that.

Pick a photo, click, and visually-close and semantically-close shots line up.
Type "sunset" or "smile" and Postra pulls them from the open folder.
No tagging required. Once initial setup is done, every new import is analyzed automatically.

Two kinds of "similar" — switchable on the fly.

Photos chasing the same subject are semantically close.
Photos taken on a different day with a similar composition are visually close.
Postra runs both through different models, and you can swap between them.

MEANING
Search by meaning (CLIP)The AI surfaces shots that match a situation: "sunset, ocean", "crosswalk, people", "rain, night".
VISUAL
Search by look (DINOv2)Use it for similar-looking shots, picking the best of a burst, grouping by color tone. You may even surface visual habits of your own you hadn't noticed.
KEYWORD
Search by keywordType natural language. Works without EXIF tags.
"Backlight", "red umbrella" — straight through.
RANDOM
Discover with randomShuffle from the library and drop into a layout. Letting randomness pick for you can surface combinations you'd never have chosen on your own.
03 · AI sizes up the "good ones"

Let the AI rough-cut your picks
before you even start choosing.

Postra scores each photo on aesthetic quality as it loads.
The top 15% of the folder lights up at a glance, so the best shots surface fast.
When you build a layout from two or more photos, color harmony is judged automatically.

A first read on "good vs. not", from the AI.

Postra tags every photo with a ★1.0〜10.0 aesthetic score as it loads.
Not absolute — color-coded as a relative ranking within the open folder, so the day's best shots float to the top.
Build a composite layout and Postra grades color harmony in Lab space across three levels — so color-clashed sets are caught before you export.

NIMA
Aesthetic score (NIMA)A MobileNet-based model rates each photo ★1.0〜10.0 for completeness. Top 15% / middle / bottom is color-coded within the folder. Runs fast on Apple Neural Engine.
HARMONY
Color harmonyWith two or more slots filled, Postra judges color cohesion in Lab space across three levels — "in tune / slightly off / mismatched". Prevents color-fractured SNS grids before export.

05 · Non-destructive retouch + color balance

The original is never touched.
Roll back and redo as many times as you like.

Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, white balance, tint, saturation, vibrance, tone curve, and split-tone color balance — all non-destructive. RAW from major formats (ARW · DNG and others) can be developed inside the app with a lightweight develop. For Fuji X-Trans (RAF), Postra uses the embedded JPEG so you can browse, lay out, and retouch right away. When you need a full-featured develop, hand off to Capture One or your preferred app. All adjustment values are saved to a sidecar; the original JPG / RAW stays untouched.

FAQ

This is a focused tool.
Please read before buying.

One-time purchase or subscription?

One-time purchase. Once you buy a version, you can keep using it even after updates stop. If a future major upgrade ships (for example 2.0), upgrade pricing will be offered to existing customers.

Is there a Windows version?

Currently Mac (Apple Silicon) only. A Windows version is something we may consider in the future, but there is no release timeline yet.

Will it run on Intel Macs?

No. Postra is built for Apple Silicon (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4 series) only, and will not launch on Intel Macs.

How to check: open "About This Mac" and look at the "Chip" or "Processor" line. If it shows Apple M1 / M2 / M3 / M4, you're good to go. If it shows Intel Core i5 / i7 / i9 / Xeon, Postra will not work on that machine.

There are no plans to support Intel Macs.

How fast is the AI analysis?

AI inference runs on the Apple Silicon Metal GPU, so it stays responsive even on M1-class machines. For a library of around 10,000 photos: CLIP alone takes about 10 minutes; including DINOv2, expect roughly 20 to 50 minutes depending on your machine (M4 Max to M1 Pro range). See the Speed section for measured numbers.

Analysis runs once and is cached. After that, searches are immediate. AI analysis can also be paused / resumed, so it's fine to leave it running overnight.

AI features are optional. "Semantic search (CLIP)" and "Visual search (DINOv2)" can each be turned on or off individually in Settings. Without AI, photo browse, culling, composite layout, and export run even more lightly.

How much RAW develop is supported?

RAW from major formats (ARW · DNG and others) can be developed inside Postra with a lightweight develop. Adjust exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, white balance, tint, saturation, vibrance, tone curve, and split-tone color balance with sliders, and export as JPEG. Presets and user-defined Styles are available, and the workflow happens on the same screen as JPG retouch.

Fuji X-Trans (RAF) native develop is not available in this version (v1.1). However, the embedded JPEG developed in-camera can be used for browsing, layout, and retouch with no friction. When a full-featured develop is needed, Postra can hand the file off to an external editor (Capture One, Lightroom, RawTherapee, and others) configured in Settings.

* Export is JPEG only. The original JPG / RAW is never overwritten.

Can I use it alongside Lightroom or Capture One?

Yes. In the current version (v1.1), Postra is designed around a JPG-first workflow. JPG and RAW from major formats can be lightweight-developed and retouched in Postra and exported as JPEG; for Fuji RAF or any case where a full-featured develop is required, Postra can hand the file off to an external editor configured in Settings.

It also fits well as: select / layout / AI search in Postra, then finish only the picks in Lightroom or Capture One.

Are my photos sent to the cloud?

No. Similarity search, keyword search, retouch, and every other feature run on-device.
A network connection is generally not required (only for the initial AI feature setup and update checks).

Can I share one library across multiple Macs via external SSD?

Yes — between Macs, you can put both photo files and the cache on an external SSD, and share thumbnails, previews, and AI analysis results (similarity-search cache) across multiple Macs. Once AI analysis has been run, plugging the SSD into another Mac doesn't require re-analysis.

How to set it up: create folders for photos and cache on the SSD, then in each Mac's Postra go to "Settings → Cache location" and point it to the SSD path.

Caveats:

  • Don't change the SSD's volume name (changing it changes the mount path, and the cache will be regenerated). Also avoid plugging in another SSD with the same volume name while one is already mounted.
  • Working state — ratings, accept / reject, retouch values, etc. — is stored locally per Mac and is not on the SSD. You can't continue selection work from Mac A on Mac B. The shared SSD setup covers "share the heavy AI analysis and image cache so the second Mac opens just as fast."
