About indexing: the AI index runs on-device and keeps progressing in the background after you close the app. It shows an estimated time remaining, and anything finished is saved even if you stop midway. Similarity search and AI score need no extra download; only word search downloads a search model (~146MB) once on first use. As a reference: on an iPhone 17, a first index of about 45,000 photos (25,000 on device + 20,000 synced from iCloud) took around 5 hours. For a large library, we recommend setting Auto-Lock to "Never" and letting it run overnight while charging.

Originals are never touched.
Redo anything, any time.

Your photo library is read-only — originals are never modified or deleted.

FAQ

The questions, answered first.

One-time purchase or subscription?

One-time. Pay $4.99 once and every feature is yours. No in-app purchases, no ads.

Will my photos be modified?

Never. The photo library is treated as read-only, and originals are never modified or deleted. Retouching only affects the in-app view and the export — you can redo it as many times as you like.

Are my photos sent to the cloud?

No. AI analysis, search, retouch, and export all run entirely on your device, and your photos are never sent anywhere. Network traffic is limited to feature downloads — the one-time search model, on-device translation data if needed, and photos that exist only in iCloud. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

What do I need to use AI search?

Just run "AI index" inside the app. Indexing happens on-device and keeps progressing in the background. Only word search requires a one-time download of the search model (~146MB) on first use; similarity search and AI score need no extra download.

What can I search for?

Plain words — "flower", "sunset", "night, street". The AI searches by meaning, so untagged photos surface too. Japanese queries also work, using a dictionary of common photo terms combined with on-device translation.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. One purchase installs on both iPhone and iPad. In landscape on iPad, the canvas and the panel sit side by side, and you can drag the divider to split the workspace.

How is it different from the Mac version?

The Mac version is file-based — it reads folders directly and supports lightweight RAW develop. The iOS version works with the iOS photo library (including iCloud Photos), read-only. The layout and color engines are verified to match across both. They are separate apps and separate purchases (the Mac version is $24.99).

What are the export options?

JPEG. Pick a long edge of 1080 / 1440 / 2048 / 2400 / 3000px. There are three output styles: "Single image", "Carousel" (numbered individual files), or "Individual". After export, the share sheet takes it to Photos, AirDrop, or any other app.

How long does AI indexing take?

It depends on your device and photo count. The app estimates the time remaining from its recent processing speed, so use that as your guide. Indexing continues in the background after you close the app, and anything finished is saved even if you stop midway. Limit the scope to an album or folder and you can start searching quickly.

Does it work with photo access set to "Selected Photos"?

Yes. Everything from picking to layout and export works within your selected photos. You can change the access level (Full Access / Selected Photos) any time in iOS Settings.
