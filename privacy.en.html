Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-03

Article 1. Introduction

The developer of Postra (hereinafter, "we" or "us"; the application is referred to as "the App") sets out below the privacy policy ("this Policy") regarding the handling of personal information of users (hereinafter, "you" or "users") of the App and this website (collectively, "the Service"). For information about the responsible operator, please refer to the Japanese Act on Specified Commercial Transactions notice (Tokushoho).

Article 2. Handling of Information in the App (Important)

The App is designed to operate fully on your device. The following categories of information are not transmitted to any third-party servers, including ours:

  • The photo files you open in the App, their thumbnails, and their metadata
  • Results of similarity search, keyword search, retouch, and composite layout
  • The contents of any files created within the App

All such processing runs locally on your device, and the App is not designed to transmit this data externally.

Article 3. Cases Where Network Communication Occurs

The App operates offline by default, and only communicates with external servers in the following limited cases:

  • AI model download on first launch: To enable similarity search (CLIP / DINOv2), the App downloads model files from a model-distribution server operated by Hugging Face. The information transmitted is limited to standard HTTP headers (IP address, User-Agent, etc.) associated with the file request; your photos are never transmitted.
  • Update checks: The App may transmit the current version information to an update-check server we operate.
  • Purchase verification: The App is sold through the Mac App Store. Purchase verification and license management are handled by Apple's Mac App Store / App Store systems; we do not operate a separate license server, and no photos or personal information are transmitted to a server we operate for this purpose.

In none of these communications are your photos or photo-related metadata transmitted.

Article 4. Information We Collect

In providing the Service, we may collect the following information:

  • Payment information at purchase: Because the App is sold through the Mac App Store, purchase processing and payment-information handling are performed by Apple Inc. (or, for customers in Japan, iTunes K.K.). We do not directly collect or retain your name, email address, credit card information, billing address, or similar details. From Apple, we receive only aggregated reports such as sales counts by region.
  • Website access information: As logs from hosting / CDN services such as Cloudflare, we may record IP address, User-Agent, referrer URL, access timestamps, and similar.
  • Information from inquiries: If you contact us via an inquiry form or email, the body of the message, your name, email address, attachments, and similar.

Article 5. Purpose of Use

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and operating the Service
  • Purchase processing and license management
  • Responding to inquiries from users
  • Investigating and addressing unauthorized access, misuse, or violations
  • Analyzing and improving usage of the Service

Article 6. Provision to Third Parties

Except as required by law, we do not provide the personal information we collect to third parties without the user's consent. However, in providing the Service, the following operators handle information in their respective roles:

  • App sales, payment processing, and distribution: Apple Inc. (United States) / for customers in Japan, iTunes K.K. (via the Mac App Store)
  • Website hosting and CDN: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States)
  • AI model distribution: Hugging Face, Inc. (United States)

The privacy policies of those operators apply to their respective handling of information.

Article 7. Security Measures

We endeavor, within reasonable means, to manage the personal information we collect to prevent unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration, leakage, and similar incidents.

Article 8. Disclosure, Correction, and Suspension of Use

You may request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of your personal information that we hold. For the procedure to make such a request, please contact the inquiry channel referenced in Article 10.

Article 9. Amendments to This Policy

We may amend this Policy as needed. Amended versions take effect from the time they are posted on this website. For material changes, we will provide notice within the App or on this website.

Article 10. Contact

For inquiries regarding this Policy, please use the contact channel on this website (to be published).
