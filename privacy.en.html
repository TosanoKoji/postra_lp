Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-19

Article 1. Introduction

The developer of Postra (hereinafter, "we" or "us"; the application is referred to as "the App") sets out below the privacy policy ("this Policy") regarding the handling of personal information of users (hereinafter, "you" or "users") of the App and this website (collectively, "the Service"). For information about the responsible operator, please refer to the Japanese Act on Specified Commercial Transactions notice (Tokushoho).

Article 2. Handling of Information in the App (Important)

The App is designed to operate fully on your device. The following categories of information are not transmitted to any third-party servers, including ours:

  • The photo files you open in the App, their thumbnails, and their metadata
  • Results of similarity search, keyword search, retouch, and composite layout
  • The contents of any files created within the App

All such processing runs locally on your device, and the App is not designed to transmit this data externally.

Article 3. Cases Where Network Communication Occurs

The App operates offline by default, and only communicates with external servers in the following limited cases:

  • AI model retrieval on first launch: To enable similarity search (CLIP / DINOv2), the App retrieves AI model files. On the Mac version, files are downloaded from a model-distribution server operated by Hugging Face. On the Android version, files are retrieved via Google Play's own delivery mechanism (Play Asset Delivery), so no third-party server other than Google is contacted. In either case, the information transmitted is limited to standard communication metadata (IP address, User-Agent, etc.) associated with the file request; your photos are never transmitted.
  • Update checks: The App may transmit the current version information to an update-check server we operate (on Android, Google Play itself handles update checks, so this communication does not occur).
  • Purchase verification: The App is sold through the Mac App Store (Mac version) or Google Play (Android version). Purchase verification and license management are handled by Apple's Mac App Store / App Store systems, or by Google Play, respectively; we do not operate a separate license server, and no photos or personal information are transmitted to a server we operate for this purpose.

In none of these communications are your photos or photo-related metadata transmitted.

Photo retrieval and mobile data use (iPhone / iPad version)

The App treats your photo library as read-only and never sends your photos anywhere. However, when working with photos stored only in iCloud (photos whose original is not on the device because of "Optimize iPhone Storage"), iOS may use data to retrieve those photos from your own iCloud. This is your photos arriving on your device from your iCloud, through Apple's system—no photos are sent to us or any third party. Data is used in the following cases, with rough amounts:

  • Photos whose original is on the device: no communication. Browsing, the board, and export all complete on-device.
  • Browsing (thumbnails): even for iCloud-only photos, small display thumbnails are fetched. These are roughly tens to a few hundred KB each and are fetched over cellular too, so the library stays usable.
  • AI indexing and search: these use only the versions already on the device and never download originals. Indexing and search themselves cause no communication. To index photos with nothing on the device, data is used only if you choose "Fetch from iCloud," and on cellular you are asked first.
  • Export and RAW development: high-quality export and RAW development need the original. If the original is not on the device, it is fetched over Wi-Fi only by default; on cellular you are asked whether to fetch it. Ordinary photos use a small search-size version of around tens of KB, while cases that need the original (such as RAW) can be tens of MB per photo.
  • Keyword-search AI model: when you use keyword search, the search model (about 146MB) is downloaded once. By default it is fetched over Wi-Fi only.
  • App and updates: downloading and updating the App from the App Store uses data like any other app.

The data-heavy actions—fetching originals and downloading the AI model—are performed over Wi-Fi only by default. You can change whether these also run over cellular at any time in the App under Settings > Photos.

Article 4. Information We Collect

In providing the Service, we may collect the following information:

  • Payment information at purchase: Because the App is sold through the Mac App Store (Mac version) or Google Play (Android version), purchase processing and payment-information handling are performed by Apple Inc. (or, for customers in Japan, iTunes K.K.) or Google LLC, respectively. We do not directly collect or retain your name, email address, credit card information, billing address, or similar details. From Apple and Google, we receive only aggregated reports such as sales counts by region.
  • Website access information: As logs from hosting / CDN services such as Cloudflare, we may record IP address, User-Agent, referrer URL, access timestamps, and similar.
  • Information from inquiries: If you contact us via an inquiry form or email, the body of the message, your name, email address, attachments, and similar.
  • Android crash/diagnostic information: Via Google Play's Android Vitals, we may receive anonymized crash logs, ANR (not-responding) reports, and aggregated device-model information through Google. This does not include your photos or personally identifying information.

Article 5. Purpose of Use

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and operating the Service
  • Purchase processing and license management
  • Responding to inquiries from users
  • Investigating and addressing unauthorized access, misuse, or violations
  • Analyzing and improving usage of the Service

Article 6. Provision to Third Parties

Except as required by law, we do not provide the personal information we collect to third parties without the user's consent. However, in providing the Service, the following operators handle information in their respective roles:

  • App sales, payment processing, and distribution: Apple Inc. (United States) / for customers in Japan, iTunes K.K. (via the Mac App Store)
  • Android app sales, payment processing, and distribution: Google LLC (United States, via Google Play)
  • Website hosting and CDN: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States)
  • AI model distribution: Hugging Face, Inc. (United States, Mac version) / Google LLC (United States, Android version via Play Asset Delivery)

The privacy policies of those operators apply to their respective handling of information.

Article 7. Security Measures

We endeavor, within reasonable means, to manage the personal information we collect to prevent unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration, leakage, and similar incidents.

Article 8. Disclosure, Correction, and Suspension of Use

Article 9. Amendments to This Policy
