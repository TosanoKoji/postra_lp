Release Notes

Postra release history, with the most recent at the top.

iPhone / iPad

Postra 1.2.0

2026-07-16 Update

Build your own layout. Adds free frame editing, frame move and fixed-aspect resize in Design, caption font choice, and favorites that recall the whole piece in one tap.

New

  • Free frame editing (Free) — leave the grid and edit each frame's position, size and ratio directly, even with a single frame. Edits auto-save to the tab, and a long-press restores the original shape.
  • Frame editing in Design — move frames and resize at a fixed aspect. The canvas ratio and editing switches now sit in a bar fixed to the bottom of the panel.
  • Caption fonts — choose from 5 typefaces: standard, Mincho, rounded gothic, typewriter and serif (Mincho and rounded gothic ship as bundled open fonts, licensed for commercial use).
  • All-in-one favorites — save the layout together with finishing touches like shadow and framing band, caption and output format under one name, and recall the whole piece in one tap.
  • PC / phone toggle for the X mock preview.

Improvements

  • Pinching the photo list (to change column count) is lighter and keeps your place.
  • Fixed caption settings being cleared when switching output targets.

Postra 1.1.1

2026-07-12 Update

Finding and choosing your photos is smoother than ever. Adds sorting in the Files tab, aperture and focal-length filters, and a per-photo mat for export.

New

  • Sorting — sort the Files tab by date taken / date edited / resolution / ISO (both ascending and descending), grouped into Date and Quality submenus, with AI-score order too.
  • Aperture and focal-length filters — combine with the existing camera, lens and orientation filters to find the shot you want fast.
  • Per-photo mat — float each photo on a mat at the target ratio when exporting individually or as a carousel.

Improvements

  • The active sort and filters now show in the status line, so you can see how things are ordered and filtered at a glance.

Postra 1.1.0

2026-07-11 Update

Straight from your set to your feed. Adds a true-to-size X carousel preview, new Carousel and Mosaic layouts, export format choice and RAW develop.

New

  • True-to-size X carousel preview — "Preview" now reproduces how an X carousel actually displays (landscape photos are center-cropped), so you can see the crop before you post.
  • Carousel layout — build a portrait/landscape mix directly and reorder by dragging; optionally add a composite cover as the first image.
  • Mosaic layout — a collage that packs each photo into a single image at its original ratio (portrait/landscape mix).
  • Export format choice and RAW develop — pick PNG / HEIC / TIFF alongside JPEG, and re-develop RAW photos on device.

Improvements

  • Redesigned the retouch panel into two columns and kept the export button always visible. Improved how background color and favorites are remembered.

Postra 1.0.2

Small refinements and fixes — the lingering indicator after iCloud export, the keyboard during search, portrait thumbnails, carousel clarity, and indexing speed on older devices.

  • Filtering to portrait-only shows taller, more legible thumbnails.

Postra 1.0.1

2026-07-02 First release

Postra is now available for iPhone and iPad. Pick, arrange, retouch and export from your camera roll in one flow — all on device.

Main features

  • Pick with AI — Recent / Random / AI text search (search by words like "flower" or "sky"), find similar photos from a single one, and filter by camera and lens.
  • Build photo sets — free layouts with grid / free / design templates, aspect-ratio presets for Instagram, X, Threads and more, and a "Preview" of how it looks on each platform.
  • Refine — from one-tap preset color grading to brightness, HSL, curves and cropping; copy and paste adjustments between photos.
  • Finish and export — a caption band with EXIF (camera, lens, aperture, date), paper texture and drop shadows for a print-like finish, and high-quality export at each platform's ratio.

Privacy

  • Analysis, layout and export all happen on device. Your original photos are never modified. No account, no tracking, no ads. (Only AI text search downloads a search model, about 146 MB, once on first use.)

System requirements

  • iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. iPhone and iPad.

Mac

Postra 1.3.0

2026-07-08 Update

Turn a set of photos into a finished piece. Print / framed-style export with frames, caption bands and paper texture, plus 38 design templates, a retouch expansion pack, and undo / redo.

New

  • Print / framed-style export — drop shadows, paper texture, a frame, a caption band beneath the photos and a film-style date stamp, plus content scaling and vertical position, so a photo set looks like a framed print.
  • 38 design templates — a new "Design" mode with 38 layouts that mix tilt and scale; build magazine-style pages in a click.
  • Retouch expansion pack — vignette and grain, 8-band HSL color adjustment, a preset-strength percentage, and "Tone Match" to unify the colors across a set of photos.
  • Undo / redo — undo arrangement and retouch with Command + Z, redo with Command + Shift + Z.
  • Export favorites / fixed output ratio — save your finishing and look settings as one recallable set, and lock the output to an exact aspect ratio.

Improvements

  • Smarter, faster AI search — a greatly expanded Japanese→English dictionary improves meaning search, and search no longer freezes the app — now with live progress.
  • Better progress feedback — AI indexing shows an estimated time remaining, and export shows progress plus a summary of any failures.

Fixes

  • Fixed zoom when dragging a design divider, a mismatch in the export size readout when the caption band is on, and HSL band-color rendering.

Postra 1.2.0

2026-06-21 Update

Easier to start, and easier to cull your way. A "Your Taste" sort that learns from your picks, a first-run guide, rate-on-thumbnail, a native menu bar, and more — a big usability pass.

New

  • "Your Taste (visual)" sorting — DINOv2 learns the look of the photos you pick or rate highly, then re-ranks your whole library toward your taste. Learning and sorting run entirely on-device.
  • Getting-started guide — a first-run guide walks you through load → cull → arrange → export, with a link to the tutorial videos.
  • Rate right on the thumbnails — hover a thumbnail to set pick/reject and star ratings in place. Optionally auto-advance to the next photo after rating (on by default for new users). A selected-photo info panel was added to the sidebar.
  • Native macOS menu bar — reach key features from the menu bar.
  • Before / after retouch compare — while you drag a retouch slider, see before and after side by side (Command + scroll works too).
  • "Current View" in the sidebar — see every active sort and filter at a glance, and clear them all in one click.

Improvements

  • Aesthetic score (NIMA) is now framed as a crowd-average yardstick, with personal preference handled by "Your Taste".
  • Redesigned right-click menu, a collapsible sidebar, and readability tweaks (contrast and button labels).

Postra 1.1.0

2026-06-18 Update

Cull and arrange large photo sets faster and more freely. A rebuilt RAW engine, search and filtering by shooting info, automatic grouping of lookalikes, and new export modes.

New

  • Rebuilt RAW engine (LibRaw) — expanded support including Fujifilm X-Trans (RAF) for in-app lightweight develop, with a tabbed develop panel.
  • Shooting info (EXIF) display — see camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed and ISO on thumbnail hover and in full-screen view.
  • Sort & Filter panel — sort by camera, lens, orientation, ISO, aperture, or color; filter by value picked from your actual data (e.g. Camera = X-T5); show only photos related to an AI keyword.
  • Similarity grouping — AI auto-groups lookalike shots (adjustable strength) to spot bursts/duplicates and speed up culling.
  • Carousel export (Beta) — export swipeable photo sets as numbered files, with shared aspect ratio, safe-area guides and per-frame adjustment.
  • Free-placement canvas (Beta) — arrange, resize and layer photos freely and export as a single composite image, with per-photo retouch and color.

Improvements

  • English localization for the new Sort & Filter panels.

Postra 1.0.2

2026-06-13 Update

A redesigned export experience and a more powerful Free mode — building photo sets is now faster and more flexible.

New

  • Unified Export window — output your layout, export individual photos, or hand RAW files to an external developer, all in one place with a live preview.
  • Five layout presets (tabs) in Free mode — switch compositions with a single click. Handy for documents, photo collages, and blog posts.
  • Rotation control in layouts — per-photo angle correction (corners filled automatically).

Improvements

  • Export presets now remember margins, spacing, and background color.
  • Clearer selection highlight on layout slots, plus optional pick/rating mini-badges.

Fixes

  • Fixed Cmd+R (reset) not working after a keyword search.
  • Fixed vertical photos appearing sideways for some RAW files (e.g., Fujifilm X-Trans).

Postra 1.0.1

2026-06-07 First release

Postra is now on sale, released for Mac (Apple Silicon) only.

Main features

  • Similarity search (CLIP: semantically similar / DINOv2: visually similar)
  • Keyword search (natural language; no tagging required)
  • A/B dual layout (compare side by side, export the one you prefer)
  • Non-destructive retouch + split-tone color balance (saved to a sidecar; original files are not modified)
  • Social presets (1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9 / 9:16)
  • AI inference on the Apple Silicon Metal GPU — responsive on M1
  • One license, installable on up to two Macs

System requirements

  • macOS 12 Monterey or later / Apple Silicon (M1 or later) required / 8 GB RAM or more (16 GB or more recommended)
  • * Will not launch on Intel Macs

Known issues

  • For very large folders (over 10,000 photos), the first AI analysis pass takes time. Analysis runs in the background and supports pause / resume.
