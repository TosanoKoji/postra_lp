Release Notes

Postra release history, with the most recent at the top.

Postra 1.2.0

2026-06-21 Update

Easier to start, and easier to cull your way. A "Your Taste" sort that learns from your picks, a first-run guide, rate-on-thumbnail, a native menu bar, and more — a big usability pass.

New

  • "Your Taste (visual)" sorting — DINOv2 learns the look of the photos you pick or rate highly, then re-ranks your whole library toward your taste. Learning and sorting run entirely on-device.
  • Getting-started guide — a first-run guide walks you through load → cull → arrange → export, with a link to the tutorial videos.
  • Rate right on the thumbnails — hover a thumbnail to set pick/reject and star ratings in place. Optionally auto-advance to the next photo after rating (on by default for new users). A selected-photo info panel was added to the sidebar.
  • Native macOS menu bar — reach key features from the menu bar.
  • Before / after retouch compare — while you drag a retouch slider, see before and after side by side (Command + scroll works too).
  • "Current View" in the sidebar — see every active sort and filter at a glance, and clear them all in one click.

Improvements

  • Aesthetic score (NIMA) is now framed as a crowd-average yardstick, with personal preference handled by "Your Taste".
  • Redesigned right-click menu, a collapsible sidebar, and readability tweaks (contrast and button labels).

Postra 1.1.0

2026-06-18 Update

Cull and arrange large photo sets faster and more freely. A rebuilt RAW engine, search and filtering by shooting info, automatic grouping of lookalikes, and new export modes.

New

  • Rebuilt RAW engine (LibRaw) — expanded support including Fujifilm X-Trans (RAF) for in-app lightweight develop, with a tabbed develop panel.
  • Shooting info (EXIF) display — see camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed and ISO on thumbnail hover and in full-screen view.
  • Sort & Filter panel — sort by camera, lens, orientation, ISO, aperture, or color; filter by value picked from your actual data (e.g. Camera = X-T5); show only photos related to an AI keyword.
  • Similarity grouping — AI auto-groups lookalike shots (adjustable strength) to spot bursts/duplicates and speed up culling.
  • Carousel export (Beta) — export swipeable photo sets as numbered files, with shared aspect ratio, safe-area guides and per-frame adjustment.
  • Free-placement canvas (Beta) — arrange, resize and layer photos freely and export as a single composite image, with per-photo retouch and color.

Improvements

  • English localization for the new Sort & Filter panels.

Postra 1.0.2

2026-06-13 Update

A redesigned export experience and a more powerful Free mode — building photo sets is now faster and more flexible.

New

  • Unified Export window — output your layout, export individual photos, or hand RAW files to an external developer, all in one place with a live preview.
  • Five layout presets (tabs) in Free mode — switch compositions with a single click. Handy for documents, photo collages, and blog posts.
  • Rotation control in layouts — per-photo angle correction (corners filled automatically).

Improvements

  • Export presets now remember margins, spacing, and background color.
  • Clearer selection highlight on layout slots, plus optional pick/rating mini-badges.

Fixes

  • Fixed Cmd+R (reset) not working after a keyword search.
  • Fixed vertical photos appearing sideways for some RAW files (e.g., Fujifilm X-Trans).

Postra 1.0.1

2026-06-07 First release

Postra is now on sale, released for Mac (Apple Silicon) only.

Main features

  • Similarity search (CLIP: semantically similar / DINOv2: visually similar)
  • Keyword search (natural language; no tagging required)
  • A/B dual layout (compare side by side, export the one you prefer)
  • Non-destructive retouch + split-tone color balance (saved to a sidecar; original files are not modified)
  • Social presets (1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9 / 9:16)
  • AI inference on the Apple Silicon Metal GPU — responsive on M1
  • One license, installable on up to two Macs

System requirements

  • macOS 12 Monterey or later / Apple Silicon (M1 or later) required / 8 GB RAM or more (16 GB or more recommended)
  • * Will not launch on Intel Macs

Known issues

  • For very large folders (over 10,000 photos), the first AI analysis pass takes time. Analysis runs in the background and supports pause / resume.
