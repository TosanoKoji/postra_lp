Release Notes

Postra release history, with the most recent at the top. For the big new features with screenshots, see What's New Highlights.

iPhone / iPad

Postra 1.7.0

2026-09-01 Major update

Free collage now has Multiple Boards. Keep several collage boards at once, switch between them to finish each one, and export them all together — handy for finishing a tall composite as a multi-image post. We also folded in feedback from real-world use, refining per-board undo history, the board thumbnails, and the control panel on iPad.

New — Multiple boards (free collage)

  • Several boards at once — keep multiple collage boards, and switch between them to finish each as its own image.
  • Export them together — export all boards at once and preview them as a multi-image post (for X and more). Great for turning a tall composite into a two-image set.
  • Move photos and reorder — send a photo to another board, reorder your boards, or apply an adjustment across all boards at once.
  • From any tab — open the board list as a collapsible strip from any tab: Select, Layout, Retouch, or Export.

From real-world feedback

  • Per-board undo history — undo history is now kept separately for each board, so moving between boards preserves each one's history.
  • Retouch and RAW in thumbnails — the board-strip thumbnails now reflect your retouch and RAW development.
  • Remembers your retouch selection — each board remembers the retouch target you had selected last.

iPad and stability

  • Resizable control panel (iPad) — narrow the canvas to widen the panel and see more at once. iPhone is now locked to portrait.
  • Fixed small issues around handling multiple boards and saving data for a more stable experience.

Postra 1.6.1

2026-08-30 Quality update

We put the new Play tab through real-world use and refined it throughout — the texture of film grain and dust, how smoothly effects ease in, and a touch of randomness so every take looks a little different. We also made the before / after comparison larger and truer to shape, and reworked how you fill the layout and split a photo.

Play, refined on real photos

  • Reworked film scratches and dust — dust that used to look like hard black squares is now made of irregular, subtly colored specks like real debris. Three layers — fine light dust, the occasional larger fleck, and dark micro-grains — give it a natural, film-like feel.
  • Smoother ramp as effects begin — effects that jumped in strength the moment you nudged the slider now ease in gently through the low-to-mid range. This applies to every effect.
  • Built-in randomness — film grain, light leaks and other effects that always drew the same pattern now vary. Reroll an individual effect to shuffle its strength and character, or keep your mix and just change its texture — more room to play.
  • Grain that reads as film — the noise-based effects (grain, high-ISO, Lith and more) are built on a film-like grain base with less of a digital-noise look.

A clearer before / after

  • Reworked comparison view — the "before / after" labels are gone, and the reclaimed space now shows your photo larger and at its true aspect ratio.
  • Comparison for Play too — while you drag a Play effect's slider, you can compare it with and without the effect side by side.

Layout and everyday use

  • Fill up to 150% — keep the spacing between photos while scaling the group up to close the borders and reach the edges (anything past the edge is cropped).
  • Splits inherit your edits — when you split one photo, its retouch and Play adjustments carry over to each band, and clearing a split is more reliable.
  • Type a ratio, and recent ratios — fixed an issue entering an aspect ratio by number, and added a "recently used" list to pick from.
  • The tap-to-enlarge view now matches the shape of the file you actually export (no rounded corners).

Postra 1.6.0

2026-08-28 Major update

A new Play tab lets you have fun with your photos — layer film grain, halation, light leaks, double exposure and other analog textures in a single tap. Over 60 effects and nearly 40 looks (presets) are ready to go, and AI search and duplication got a round of polish too.

New — the Play tab

  • A fifth tab for finishing photos — sitting between Retouch and Export, a place to “play” with your photos. Effects stack, come off anytime, and the preview always matches the export.
  • Over 60 effects — film grain (with a true silver-halide feel), halation and bloom, light leaks, lens flare, double exposure, prism, film scratches and dust, mold, film looks like redscale, infrared and cross-process, toning, and cinematic grades. Analog texture in one tap.
  • Nearly 40 looks (presets) — pick one from the thumbnail sheet and the mood is set — from film-stock styles and cinematic grades to pinhole and fine print.
  • Fine control, or leave it to chance — dial in the strength and parameters of each effect, or let it suggest random combinations.
  • Film mounts and a date stamp — composite a film strip or a contact sheet, plus a retro seven-segment date stamp.
  • Copy & paste effects — take a combination you like and apply it to another photo in one move.
  • More ways to duplicate — long-press a board tile to duplicate it next door, and optionally add random effects when duplicating across a carousel or grid.

Improvements

  • AI search stays fast — fixed search slowing down after the first query, so results appear quickly every time.
  • Duplication aspect ratio — fixed an issue where duplicating in some layouts changed a photo’s aspect ratio (crop).
  • Paper texture — tuned so the matte-paper feel is back to its intended, subtle finish.
  • Renamed the Export tab for consistency.

Postra 1.5.0

2026-08-19 Update

From new ways to show your work to faster search and safer handling of data and storage. Photo Split and a carousel preview join a much faster AI search, and photos stored only in iCloud are now fetched over Wi-Fi by default to prevent unexpected cellular data use.

New

  • Photo Split — divide one photo into up to four vertical strips for a carousel. Easy to undo back to the single photo.
  • Carousel view in the X preview — arrange against the real layout your post will have.
  • Before / after in Retouch — compare as you drag, and see which adjustments are active at a glance.
  • Faster AI search, more results — results appear almost instantly, and more load as you scroll (previously only the top matches were shown).

Data and storage you can trust

  • iCloud photos fetched over Wi-Fi by default — originals of photos stored only in iCloud are fetched only on Wi-Fi by default, preventing unexpected cellular data use (adjustable in Settings). For exactly when data is used, see About communication.
  • Automatic temp-file cleanup — temporary files from exports are cleaned up automatically, including leftovers from earlier versions, collected at launch.
  • AI indexing runs only when you start it — and you choose whether it continues in the background.

Improvements

  • Deleting a photo in the Photos app is now reflected right away in the picker and on the board.
  • You can now cancel an export while it is running.
  • Added an in-app way to add photos when using Selected Photos access.
  • Fixed a carousel export issue, with broad stability improvements and a careful review of how photo data is handled.

Postra 1.3.0

2026-07-19 Update

Faster photo culling. Adds ratings — stars, pick/hold/reject and color tags with instant filtering — plus richer caption styling and more export freedom.

New

  • Ratings — long-press a thumbnail to assign ★1–5, pick/hold/reject and 5 color tags. Filter instantly from the ☆ button, and use “Select multiple” to batch-apply or clear. Ratings are stored on your device and never written to your photo library.
  • Independent caption styles — style the band title and shooting info separately (font, size, color, opacity, alignment, line spacing). Long shooting-info lines wrap automatically, and exports match the preview line for line.
  • “Original” long edge — export individual photos and carousels at the source resolution, with no upscaling.
  • “Camera photos only” — a new setting that hides images without shooting metadata (EXIF), such as screenshots and AI-generated images, from the photo list.

Improvements

  • The photo list now remembers your scroll position when you leave the tab and return.
  • Fixed the “Analyzing…” progress occasionally lingering on the Lock Screen after indexing completes.

Postra 1.0.1

2026-07-02 First release

Postra is now available for iPhone and iPad. Pick, arrange, retouch and export from your camera roll in one flow — all on device.

Main features

  • Pick with AI — Recent / Random / AI text search (search by words like "flower" or "sky"), find similar photos from a single one, and filter by camera and lens.
  • Build photo sets — free layouts with grid / free / design templates, aspect-ratio presets for Instagram, X, Threads and more, and a "Preview" of how it looks on each platform.
  • Refine — from one-tap preset color grading to brightness, HSL, curves and cropping; copy and paste adjustments between photos.
  • Finish and export — a caption band with EXIF (camera, lens, aperture, date), paper texture and drop shadows for a print-like finish, and high-quality export at each platform's ratio.

Privacy

  • Analysis, layout and export all happen on device. Your original photos are never modified. No account, no tracking, no ads. (Only AI text search downloads a search model, about 146 MB, once on first use.)

System requirements

  • iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. iPhone and iPad.

Postra 1.5.0

2026-09-01 Major update

A new "Play" tab lets you play with the finish. Layer analog textures — glow, film tones, grain, color grading — over your originals without touching the photo. 59 effects in all, plus 38 one-tap Looks. Add multi-board for finishing several sets in parallel and a reworked way to pick layouts, and this update meaningfully widens what you can build.

New — the "Play" tab

  • A non-destructive finishing layer — Added "Play" right next to Retouch. Layer effects over your originals without touching the photo or your retouch. Remove them anytime, and preview always matches the export.
  • 59 effects — Glow effects like halation and bloom, film-stock color grading, silver-grain-inspired grain, and art / optical looks. Each is adjustable by slider, with a color picker for effects that take a color.
  • 38 built-in Looks — Pick from a photo-based thumbnail gallery to apply several effects at once, from film to cinematic, and land your mood in a tap.
  • Surprise me & applied badge — Roll a random combination with "Surprise me." A badge shows what's applied at a glance, and Undo (Command + Z) rolls it back instantly.
  • Before / after while adjusting — See the effect on and off side by side as you drag, and reset the whole stack in one tap.

New — multi-board (free boards)

  • Multiple boards at once — Keep several composite "boards," each with its own photos, layout and settings, and finish each one as its own piece as you switch between them.
  • Real-image board thumbnails — Every board thumbnail shows an actual photo, so you can tell them apart at a glance.
  • Scale to survey — Scale a board's contents down to take in the whole thing, and the caption band height is now adjustable and wraps.

New — reworked layout modes

  • First-class layout switching — Grid, Free, Design and Carousel now sit together as a "layout" row for easy switching, with grid variants tucked into a submenu.
  • Remembered output type — The carousel output type (composite / carousel / individual) is remembered and restored next time.
  • Free-edit frames on the board — Drag layout frames directly on the board to resize and reposition them freely.
  • Filter by orientation — Added a portrait / landscape / square filter to gather just the orientation you want.

Refinements

  • Refined the export call-to-action colors so the next button to press is obvious.
  • The frozen retouch / export color pipeline is untouched — Play is added as an independent, non-destructive layer.

Main features

  • Similarity search (CLIP: semantically similar / DINOv2: visually similar)
